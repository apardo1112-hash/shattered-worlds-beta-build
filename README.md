*Shattered Worlds* is a medieval sci-fi fantasy game set in a world where ancient magic and futuristic technology have collided. Long ago, a mysterious event shattered the planet into floating realms, each with its own kingdoms, creatures, and dangerous environments. Players explore ruined castles, enchanted forests, abandoned futuristic cities, and massive floating islands while fighting knights, monsters, robots, and powerful magical beings. Swords, shields, bows, and magic can be combined with advanced weapons and mysterious technology discovered throughout the world.

Players take on the role of a young warrior who must travel across the shattered realms to uncover what caused the world to break apart. Along the journey, they can choose to help different kingdoms, discover ancient secrets, battle powerful bosses, and collect new weapons and abilities. The choices players make can change relationships between factions and influence how the story unfolds. With exploration, combat, crafting, magic, and science-fiction technology, *Shattered Worlds* combines the adventure of a medieval fantasy game with the mystery and excitement of a futuristic sci-fi world.
